Solutions

01 / 04

Solar fields, wind farms and transmission corridors stretch further than any crew can walk in a season. One autonomous pass maps every row, blade and span, and flags whatever has moved since the last flight.

Bridges, viaducts and towers carry their loads every day, often over water, rail or open road. We measure deflection and surface wear to the millimetre while the traffic keeps moving underneath.

Plants, tank farms and stockyards change shape every shift and rarely stop for an audit. Scheduled flights record tanks, racks and piles in a single sweep, so inspection becomes routine instead of a shutdown.

Slopes, coastlines and forests move quietly between survey seasons. Multi-echo pulses see through the canopy to bare ground, giving you a fresh elevation model in a day.
