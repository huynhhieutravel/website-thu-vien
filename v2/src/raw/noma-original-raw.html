(06)The Process

How we carry every project from brief to launchday.

Nothing falls through the cracks, because there are no cracks. One team, one plan, one room.

You get one point of contact and one shared timeline. Updates arrive before you think to ask for them.

P.(01)Week 1
  • Kickoff call
  • Audience and market read
  • Audit of what you have
  • Written brief you sign off

Discovery.

One call, one sharp brief. Your goals, your audience, and what's in the way.

P.(02)Week 2
  • Positioning and voice
  • One art direction, not three
  • Messaging framework
  • Sitemap and page intent

Strategy.

One creative direction, argued for and agreed — before a single pixel moves.

P.(03)Weeks 3 to 5
  • Identity system
  • Every page designed
  • Build and motion pass
  • Three rounds of revisions

Design.

Brand and site designed, then built. Three rounds, sharp ones.

P.(04)Week 6
  • Testing across devices
  • Analytics and metadata
  • Every file and license handed over
  • Thirty days of support

Launch.

We ship, hand over the keys, and stay close for thirty days after go-live.
