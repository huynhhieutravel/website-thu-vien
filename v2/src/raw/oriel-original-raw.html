Around people

Calm company, by design.

Oriel moves at a human pace, speaks softly and never hovers. The aim is simple: after a week, you stop noticing it's there.

  • Keeps a comfortable distance

    About an arm and a half away, closer only when you ask.

  • Slows down around children and pets

    And steps around a sleeping dog rather than over it.

  • Says what it is about to do

    A soft word before it reaches, lifts or opens anything.

  • Pauses at closed doors

    It asks before it goes into a room that has been shut.

Safety and privacy

Soft, slow, and always listening for stop.

A robot that shares your hallway has to be gentle before it is clever. These limits are built into the hardware, not added as settings.

  • Yields on contact

    Force-limited joints soften the instant they meet unexpected resistance, and stop within 50 ms.

  • Walks at a human pace

    Speed is capped indoors, and lower still near children, pets and stairs.

  • Private by design

    Vision and voice are processed on board. A physical shutter closes the cameras whenever you like.

  • Asks before it acts

    Unfamiliar objects, closed doors and new chores all get a question first.

  • Stops on a word

    Say “Oriel, stop”, press the button between its shoulders, or tap stop in the app.

  • Expert help, only with you

    For a job it hasn’t learned, a trained specialist can guide it remotely, only in a session you approve.
