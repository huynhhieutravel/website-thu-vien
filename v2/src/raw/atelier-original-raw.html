01 — Project

Product Design

Lume Eyewear Launch

Eyewear that reads as an object first, a product second.

Overview

Naming, packaging, and launch system for an independent eyewear house. Every frame ships in a fold-flat case designed to sit on a shelf like a small sculpture.

Brief

Challenge

Stand out in a category owned by two conglomerates and a thousand lookalikes.

Direction

Lead with the silhouette. Strip the packaging to one colour and one line of type. Let the lens do the talking.

Outcome

Sold through the launch colourway in three weeks. Picked up by two concept stores in Copenhagen.
