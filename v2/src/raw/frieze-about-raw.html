About

Est. 2016

Frieze is a design studio working in identity, typeface design, editorial, packaging and wayfinding. We photograph most of it ourselves.

We make things that reward a second look.

A studio of nine, working between London and Lisbon. Six projects a year, each seen through from the first reading to the last proof.

01Position

Most work is made to be scanned. We are interested in the other kind.

The sign you read twice because the arrow sits at exactly the right weight. The book whose margin gives the photograph room to breathe. The label you keep after the bottle is empty. None of that happens by accident, and none of it survives a process built for speed.

So we work slowly and we work close. A project usually starts with a week of reading and ends with a week of proofing. The part in between is mostly subtraction.

03Method

Three movements, in order, every time. The order is the method.

01Movement

Read

Before anything is drawn we read everything: the brief, the archive, the complaints, the competition. Most of what a project needs is already written down somewhere by someone who was annoyed.

02Movement

Draw

We draw far more than we show. The work you see is the third or fourth position, and it survives because it beat the others in the room, not because it arrived first.

03Movement

Prove

Nothing leaves the studio untested. Type gets proofed on paper, signage gets printed at size and taped to a wall, interfaces get handed to someone who did not make them.
